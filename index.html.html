THE SEASON CHRONICLES

The Lost Language of Nature

A letter by lantern light

“Traveler — the Eternal Tree is fading. A force called the Silence is erasing the words for flowers, rain, snow and wind. Four fragments of the language of nature remain, one in each season. Will you find them? — Elyra”

En resumen: el Árbol Eterno se marchita porque el Silencio borra las palabras de la naturaleza. Cada palabra que aprendas le devuelve vida.
